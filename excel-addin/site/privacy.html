Privacy

Estimate Navigator, an Excel add-in

Where it runs

Estimate Navigator runs entirely inside Excel.

What it reads and writes

It reads the open workbook to show its index, checks and codes. It writes to the workbook only when you click Use or an Insert button.

What it collects

  • It collects no personal data.
  • It sends no data anywhere.
  • It uses no cookies and no analytics.
  • It makes no network requests, except to load its own files and brand from anvilrockroad.com, and Microsoft's Office.js library from Microsoft's servers, which every Office add-in loads.
  • It stores nothing outside the workbook.
